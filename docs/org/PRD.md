## 2. ACTIVE PRODUCT PIPELINE

### 📍 Product 1: TrackRide — Group & Tour Navigation (Anchor Product)

- **Domain:** B2C Travel / Navigation / Safety
- **Core Concept:** A voice-guided, motorcycle-focused navigation and group awareness app for solo and touring riders. Tracks all group members within a set route radius, alerts when members deviate from the route or stall, and provides offline-first navigation and nearby emergency POIs (fuel, hospital, service).
- **Monetization Model:**
  - _Free Tier:_ Single rider (voice navigation, offline route tracking, POIs, deviation alert).
  - _Paid Tier:_ Host-paid group ride model (Host pays ₹300 per group ride up to 5 members; guest riders join free).
- **Status:** **Active Development (Flagship Product — Target Release: January 2027)**

---

### 👕 Product 2: Custom Apparel & AI Print E-Commerce

- **Domain:** B2C E-Commerce / AI Customization
- **Core Concept:** A digital custom clothing platform where users select blank apparel (t-shirts, hoodies), generate unique custom artwork using generative AI prompts, preview mockups in real-time, and place orders. Orders are automatically routed to local printing/customization partners for print-on-demand fulfillment and direct delivery.
- **Key Integrations:** AI image generation engine, local print seller fulfillment network API, e-commerce checkout.
- **Status:** Pipeline Concept

---

### 👥 Product 3: HR & Automated Candidate Interview Platform

- **Domain:** B2B HRTech / Hiring Automation
- **Core Concept:** An automated recruitment platform that streamlines candidate screening, asynchronous video/coding assessments, and structured interview workflows for modern engineering and operations teams.
- **Status:** Research / Concept Stage
